Kernel-levelHoneypot / FakeNetFilter.sys -- kd command reference
================================================================
Run in the kd session attached to the guest (kd -k net:port=<PORT>,key=<KEY>).
Lines beginning with ">" are typed at the kd prompt.

--- attach / sanity ---
> .reload
> vertarget                       ; confirm this is the DISPOSABLE guest
> lm m FakeNetFilter              ; is the driver loaded?

--- Driver Verifier (in guest console, then reboot, before load) ---
  verifier /standard /driver FakeNetFilter.sys
> !verifier 3 FakeNetFilter.sys

--- after a bugcheck ---
> !analyze -v
> kb
> !devobj FakeNetFilter           ; device object / queue state
> !irp <addr>                     ; inspect a pending IRP (from !analyze)
> !pool <addr>                    ; pool block triage

--- confirm the EXPORT_LOGS buffered-IOCTL fix ---
  smoke-test.ps1 issues IOCTL_HONEYPOT_EXPORT_LOGS. Before the fix the handler
  only ran under Irp->MdlAddress (always NULL for METHOD_BUFFERED) and no-opped
  while returning success. Confirm the export path now actually executes (log
  file written / status reflects real work), not a silent success.

--- do NOT enable inline hooks ---
  InstallInlineHook / HookedNtOpenProcess are experimental and NOT WIRED; their
  stubs never chain to the original syscall. If you see them installed, the
  guest will hang -- revert to the checkpoint.

--- resume / detach ---
